We carried out the development in stages, from STAGE 1 to STAGE 5.
The Jupyter Notebook filenames are labeled according to these stages and steps, allowing each phase to be reviewed independently. In the filenames—formatted as `STAGE X stepY.Z.ipynb`—`Y` represents the step number, and `Z` denotes the iteration version.

Main purpose of each stage:
STAGE 1: Track facial feature points.
STAGE 2 step1: Extract and classify eye features from video.
STAGE 2 step2: Train, validate and test the eye SVM model.
STAGE 2 step3: Verify the eye predictions on video.
STAGE 3 step1: Extract and classify mouth features from video.
STAGE 3 step2: Train, validate and test the mouth SVM model.
STAGE 3 step3: Verify the mouth predictions on video.
STAGE 4: Skipped.
STAGE 5 step0: Test VRM expressions.
STAGE 5 step1: Drive VRM expressions from video (mouth open or closed).
STAGE 5 step1.1: Drive VRM expressions from video (six mouth states).
STAGE 5 step-extra: Drive VRM expressions from video (three mouth states).
STAGE 6: Drive VRM expressions and head rotation from live video.
